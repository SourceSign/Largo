## Description (max. 4000)
Largo helps you learn songs by ear. Open a song, and Largo shows you its waveform, chords, key, tempo and bars – then lets you slow it down, change its key and repeat any part until you've got it.

SLOW IT DOWN, KEEP THE PITCH
Play a song anywhere from a quarter to twice its speed. The pitch stays the same, so a fast solo sounds just like the original – only slower. Pick a practice speed with one tap, or fine-tune it on the slider.

SEE THE CHORDS AS THEY PLAY
Largo listens to the song and detects its chords, key and tempo. The chords scroll by under the waveform in time with the music, with bar numbers, so you always know where you are and what comes next.

LOOP THE HARD PART
Drag across the waveform to make a loop. Drag its edges to adjust it, or drag it to move it. Largo repeats that passage seamlessly, as often as you need.

PLAY IN YOUR KEY
Transpose a song up or down in semitones to match your voice or your capo position – the chords change with it. Fine-tune in cents to match a recording that isn't tuned to A440.

PICKS UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
Largo remembers every song: your speed, key, loop and position are still there the next time you open it.

DESIGNED FOR PRACTICE
• A scrolling waveform with the playhead in the middle, like a tape machine
• Bar lines and bar numbers, with the time signature detected automatically
• Jump back or forward five seconds with one tap
• Keyboard shortcuts on Mac and iPad
• Background playback and AirPlay on iPhone and iPad
• Light and dark mode

ON MAC, IPHONE AND IPAD
Largo looks and feels at home on every device: a compact player on iPhone, and a large waveform with a command bar on iPad and Mac.

YOUR MUSIC STAYS YOURS
Everything happens on your device. Largo works offline and collects no data.

WORKS WITH YOUR FILES
Open MP3, M4A (AAC), WAV, AIFF, FLAC and CAF files from the Files app, iCloud Drive or the Finder. A demo song is included to try it right away. Songs from the Apple Music subscription are copy-protected and can't be opened in any app.

Whether you play guitar, bass, piano or sing – Largo turns "how does that go?" into "got it".
